# Community Code of Conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our pledge

We are committed to an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body characteristics, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Prefer the long-term interests of the community and its users.
- Protect other people's privacy in logs, screenshots, and examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, threats, stalking, or sustained harassment.
- Discriminatory, sexualized, or otherwise unwelcome language or content.
- Publishing another person's identity, contact information, credentials, or private data without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, repositories, infrastructure, or security-reporting processes.
- Other conduct that maintainers reasonably consider inappropriate in a professional collaboration space.

## Enforcement

Ordinary collaboration concerns may be raised in an issue or pull request. For harassment, threats, privacy, or security incidents, contact the maintainers through GitHub's private security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of reporters and affected people and will apply measures appropriate to the impact.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and public spaces where someone represents the GPU Ops community.
